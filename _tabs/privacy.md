# Privacy Notice

This website uses **Waline** to provide its comment system. Comments are
processed by a Waline service hosted on **Vercel** and stored in a **Neon
PostgreSQL** database.

## Information collected

When you leave a comment, you are required to provide a nickname and an email
address. Your email address is used only for identification and comment
management. It is not displayed publicly.

Waline may also process technical information such as your IP address, browser
and device information for security, moderation, and spam prevention.

## How the information is used

Information associated with comments is used only to:

- display and manage comments;
- identify comment authors;
- moderate abuse and prevent spam; and
- respond to requests concerning a comment.

Comment information is not sold by this website.

## Comment removal

You may request that your comment and its associated information be removed.
Please contact me through my [GitHub profile](https://github.com/YannieLee) and
include the page and comment you want removed.

By submitting a comment, you acknowledge that the information described above
will be processed for the operation and moderation of the comment system.
